Leader line specification — bent, solid, arrowed

Solid line at 75% of boundary line width. One bend to minimise label-to-beacon distance. Arrowhead pointing at beacon, stopping just short of the circle. Label rendered horizontally (0°) in wider whitespace.

Bend geometry — how it's computed

  1. Horizontal stub — a short fixed-length segment extends from the label's nearest edge horizontally, anchoring the text to the leader. Length = ~2× label height.
  2. Bend point selection — the algorithm picks the bend location that minimises total leader length while keeping all segments inside the polygon. Tries candidate bend points along a horizontal line swept between the label and the beacon.
  3. Second bend (if needed) — if a single diagonal from bend₁ to the beacon would exit the polygon, a second bend is added, producing a Z-shape or S-shape path that stays wholly inside the parcel.
  4. Arrowhead clearance — the final segment terminates at beacon_centre − (beacon_radius + 1pt) so the arrow tip stops just outside the circle, never overlapping it.
  5. Max bends = 2 — beyond two bends the leader becomes harder to trace; font is reduced instead and label is moved closer.
