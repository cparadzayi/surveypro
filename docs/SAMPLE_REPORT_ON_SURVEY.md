## 3. Found beacons—

**(a) Particular circumstances:**

The following existing beacons were found and examined:

- **Beacon P1:** Found in good condition. 50mm iron pipe in concrete, centre-mark intact. No particular circumstances to report.

- **Beacon P2:** Found in fair condition. Concrete monument with centre-mark visible but weathered. Surrounded by scattered stones, likely from original placement.

- **Beacon P3:** Found disturbed. 50mm iron pipe tilted approximately 15° from vertical. No centre-mark visible. Concreted by property owner during fence construction in 2023.

- **Beacon P4:** Not found. Extensive search conducted within 5-meter radius of calculated position. Area recently cleared for development. Beacon presumed destroyed during earthworks.

- **Beacon P5:** Found in good condition. Wooden peg replaced with 50mm iron pipe in concrete as per Section 4 below.

**(b) Alignment tests with results:**

The following alignment tests were performed to verify beacon positions:

- **Line P1-P2:** Alignment test performed using total station from P1 to P2 via intermediate point IP1. Measured bearing: 45°23'15". Calculated bearing from coordinates: 45°23'18". Discrepancy: 3" (0.008m at mid-point). **Result: ACCEPTABLE**

- **Line P2-P3:** Alignment test performed from P2 to P3 via IP2. Measured distance: 45.678m. Calculated distance: 45.690m. Discrepancy: 0.012m. **Result: ACCEPTABLE**

- **Line P3-P4:** Alignment test could not be performed as Beacon P4 was not found (see Section 4).

- **Line P1-P5:** Alignment test performed. Measured bearing: 135°12'45". Calculated bearing: 135°12'48". Discrepancy: 3" (0.007m). **Result: ACCEPTABLE**

**Lines adopted:**

- **P1-P2: ADOPTED** - Discrepancy within acceptable tolerance (±0.015m for cadastral surveys per SI 727).

- **P2-P3: ADOPTED** - Discrepancy within acceptable tolerance. Beacon P3 position accepted despite being concreted by owner, as alignment test confirms original position.

- **P3-P4: NOT ADOPTED** - Beacon P4 not found. New beacon placed at calculated position (see Section 4).

- **P1-P5: ADOPTED** - Discrepancy within acceptable tolerance.

**Summary:** Of 5 beacons searched, 4 were found. 3 beacons were adopted after alignment tests. 1 beacon (P4) was replaced due to not being found.

## 4. Replaced beacons: Give reasons for choice of position.

**Beacon P4:**

Original beacon not found (see Section 3). New beacon placed at calculated position determined by the following method:

- **Position determination:** Intersection from adopted beacons P1, P2, and P3 using total station observations.
- **Verification:** Position verified by RTK GPS observation (5-minute occupation).
- **Coordinates:** Y=97563.567, X=2247119.890 (Cape Lo 29°)
- **Discrepancy from calculated position:** 0.003m (within tolerance)
- **Beacon type:** 50mm iron pipe in concrete, 600mm depth, with concrete collar at surface.
- **Reason for position:** Calculated position adopted as original beacon could not be found. Position verified by multiple independent observations. Area recently developed, new beacon placed to avoid existing building foundation (2m offset from calculated position would have placed beacon under building slab).

**Beacon P5:**

Original wooden peg found deteriorated and unstable. Replaced with permanent monument.

- **Position determination:** Original position verified by alignment tests (see Section 3b).
- **Coordinates:** Y=97575.234, X=2247115.678 (Cape Lo 29°)
- **Discrepancy:** 0.000m (beacon replaced at exact original position)
- **Beacon type:** 50mm iron pipe in concrete, 600mm depth.
- **Reason for position:** Original position adopted as alignment tests confirmed accuracy. Wooden peg replaced with permanent monument to ensure long-term stability.

All replaced beacons comply with monumentation standards specified in SI 727 of 1979, Section 45.

## 6. Unusual occurrences and any other comment.

The following observations are noted:

1. **Weather conditions:** Survey conducted during dry season (October 2025). Excellent visibility throughout. No weather-related delays.

2. **Access:** Access to Beacon P3 was temporarily restricted due to ongoing construction on adjacent property. Permission obtained from property owner for survey access.

3. **Existing developments:** All new beacons were placed according to existing developments. The existing developments (boundary walls and fences) were found to correspond with the proposed subdivision layout plan SP/2018/456.

4. **Accuracy achieved:** Traverse closure precision of 1:47,500 achieved, exceeding the minimum requirement of 1:10,000 for cadastral surveys per SI 727.

5. **Total area:** Total area surveyed: 1,234.56 m² (0.1235 hectares). Area computed by coordinate method using adjusted coordinates.

6. **Subdivision:** Property subdivided into three stands:
   - Stand 123A: 412.34 m²
   - Stand 123B: 410.11 m²
   - Stand 123C: 412.11 m²
