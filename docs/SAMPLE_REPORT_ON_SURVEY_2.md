## 3. Found beacons—

**(a) Particular circumstances:**

The following farm beacons from previous survey SR/2018/1234 were found:

- **Beacon FB1:** Found in excellent condition. Concrete monument with brass plate, centre-mark clearly visible. No particular circumstances to report.

- **Beacon FB2:** Found in good condition. 50mm iron pipe in concrete. Centre-mark intact. Beacon partially obscured by vegetation, cleared for observation.

- **Beacon FB3:** Found in poor condition. Concrete monument cracked and tilted. Centre-mark visible but monument unstable. Fence post erected 0.5m from beacon by farmer.

- **Beacon FB4:** Found in fair condition. Iron pipe with concrete surround. No centre-mark visible due to concrete weathering. Position verified by alignment tests.

All farm boundary beacons were found and verified. No beacons were missing.

**(b) Alignment tests with results:**

The following alignment tests were performed:

- **Line FB1-FB2:** Alignment test performed using total station. Measured bearing: 87°45'23". Previous survey bearing (SR/2018/1234): 87°45'25". Discrepancy: 2" (0.005m at mid-point). **Result: ACCEPTABLE**

- **Line FB2-FB3:** Alignment test performed. Measured distance: 234.567m. Previous survey distance: 234.573m. Discrepancy: 0.006m. **Result: ACCEPTABLE**

- **Line FB3-FB4:** Alignment test performed. Measured bearing: 178°23'12". Previous survey bearing: 178°23'15". Discrepancy: 3" (0.007m). **Result: ACCEPTABLE**

- **Line FB1-FB4:** Check measurement performed. Measured distance: 456.789m. Calculated distance from coordinates: 456.795m. Discrepancy: 0.006m. **Result: ACCEPTABLE**

**Lines adopted:**

- **FB1-FB2: ADOPTED** - Excellent agreement with previous survey. Discrepancy well within tolerance.

- **FB2-FB3: ADOPTED** - Despite poor beacon condition, alignment test confirms original position. Discrepancy acceptable.

- **FB3-FB4: ADOPTED** - Good agreement with previous survey.

- **FB1-FB4: ADOPTED** - Check measurement confirms network integrity.

**Summary:** All 4 farm beacons from previous survey SR/2018/1234 were found and adopted after alignment tests. Network integrity confirmed. All discrepancies within acceptable tolerance (±0.015m).

## 4. Replaced beacons: Give reasons for choice of position.

**No beacons were replaced in this survey.**

All existing farm beacons from previous survey SR/2018/1234 were found in acceptable condition and adopted (see Section 3).

New servitude beacons (S1-S8) were placed at calculated positions along the servitude centerline. These are new beacons, not replacements. Positions determined by:

- Offset measurements from farm boundary (10m offset from FB2-FB3 line)
- Total station traverse along servitude centerline
- RTK GPS verification at each beacon position
- All new beacons are 50mm iron pipes in concrete, 600mm depth

## 5. Curvilinear boundaries plotted from—

**(b) ☑ taped traverse** along existing sewer pipeline route;

**(e) ☑ various methods:** Combination of taped traverse and total station tacheometric measurements;

(a) ☐ previous survey: give S.R. No. and letter reference permitting adoption;

(c) ☐ tacheometric traverse;

(d) ☐ aerial photography.

**Details of curvilinear boundary:**

The servitude has one curvilinear boundary section between beacons S3 and S5, following the existing sewer pipeline route around a natural rock outcrop.

**Method used:**
- Centerline of curve established by taped traverse along existing pipeline
- Measurements taken at 5-meter intervals along curve
- Total of 12 intermediate points measured
- Curve fitted using least squares method
- Radius of curve: 45.5m
- Arc length: 38.7m
- Chord bearing: 145°23'12"
- Chord length: 37.8m

**Verification:**
- Curve checked by total station tacheometric measurements
- All intermediate points within ±0.02m of fitted curve
- Curve plotted on plan at scale 1:500

The curvilinear boundary ensures the servitude follows the existing pipeline alignment while maintaining the required 3-meter width on each side of centerline.

## 6. Unusual occurrences and any other comment.

The following observations are noted:

1. **Pipeline location:** Existing sewer pipeline was located using ground-penetrating radar (GPR) to verify depth and alignment before beacon placement. Pipeline depth ranges from 1.2m to 2.5m below ground surface.

2. **Rock outcrop:** Natural rock outcrop between S3 and S5 necessitated curved alignment. Straight alignment would have required extensive blasting. Curved alignment approved by City of Masvingo (Ref: CM/SER/2025/089).

3. **Vegetation:** Dense vegetation along pipeline route required clearing for survey access. Clearing conducted with landowner permission. Vegetation to be restored after servitude registration.

4. **Existing services:** Existing water pipeline crosses sewer line at beacon S6. Clearance verified as adequate (1.5m vertical separation). No conflict with proposed servitude.

5. **Access arrangements:** Permanent access road to servitude (4m width) surveyed and included in servitude area. Access road connects to existing farm road at beacon S1.

6. **Servitude area:** Total servitude area: 2,456.78 m² (0.2457 hectares)
   - Straight sections: 2,234.56 m²
   - Curved section: 222.22 m²
   - Access road: (included in total)

7. **Servitude width:** 6 meters (3m each side of pipeline centerline) throughout, except at curved section where width varies from 6m to 7.5m to accommodate curve geometry.

8. **Accuracy achieved:** Traverse closure precision of 1:52,000 achieved for servitude centerline traverse.

9. **Landowner consultation:** Survey conducted with full cooperation of landowner (Mr. J. Ncube). Landowner present during beacon placement to verify positions do not interfere with existing farming operations.

10. **Compliance:** Survey conducted in full compliance with:
    - SI 727 of 1979 (Land Survey General Regulations)
